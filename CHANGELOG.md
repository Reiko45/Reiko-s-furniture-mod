# Changelog

## 1.4.0

2,799 furniture items in twenty-three sections (up from 1,459 in seventeen), eight furniture styles
(up from four), and new ways to change, light and share your furniture.

### New features
- **Repaint and reupholster:** sneak and right-click a placed piece with planks of another wood, a dye
  or wool, or a style's material to change its wood, color or style. It keeps its contents.
- **Wood stain:** sneak and right-click wooden furniture with a dye to stain it; a sponge takes it off.
  Stains stay on broken pieces and on pieces moved with the dolly.
- **Light switches:** link up to 32 lamps to a switch and turn them all on and off at once, without
  redstone.
- **Mailboxes:** send items to other players, even when they are offline. The flag goes up when mail
  is waiting.
- **Craft from the catalog:** the catalog's Craft button makes a piece from the materials in your
  inventory (shift-click to make a stack).

### New rooms
- **Greenhouse & garden:** growing pots where plants grow bigger in four stages, plant stands, hanging
  planters, window flower boxes, a two-block-tall garden shed cabinet and a **watering can** that waters
  a 3 by 3 area.
- **Home theater:** a 3 by 2 projector screen and a projector with three films, recliners that tilt
  back, speakers that send out notes while a jukebox plays nearby, a popcorn machine (popcorn is a new food)
  and movie posters.
- **Art studio:** easels and canvases you paint pixel by pixel and sign, then hang on a wall or put in
  a picture frame; a pottery wheel that makes vases, a sewing machine that makes floor cushions, and
  paint pots.
- **Holidays:** a Christmas tree with twinkling lights, presents that keep their contents, string
  lights that join into long strands, a wreath, stockings and a snow globe; for Halloween a gravestone,
  a fake cobweb, hanging bats, a candy bowl and a witch's cauldron.
- **Stone & concrete:** dining tables, coffee tables, benches, counters and planters in white and black
  marble, quartz, blackstone and stone bricks, and tables, benches, counters and planters in all 16
  concrete colors.
- **Copper & iron:** copper lamps, tables and chairs that age like copper blocks (wax them with
  honeycomb, scrape them with an axe), and a wrought-iron garden chair, table, bench and lamp post.

### Furniture styles
- **Medieval, Victorian, Futuristic and Beach House**, made from iron bars, a candle, glowstone dust
  and sand.
- **Six more styled pieces in every style:** desk, bookshelf, table lamp, floor lamp, kitchen counter
  and upper cabinet. That makes sixteen styled pieces in eight styles.

### Other mods
- **Jade and WTHIT:** tooltips show appliance progress, owners, mail, linked lamps, fish, plant growth,
  paintings and wood stain.
- **Create:** furniture moves on contraptions with its contents, and lamps stay lit.
- **Sodium and Iris:** checked; everything renders, including glowing parts and wood stain.

### Polish
- **Eight new advancements:** You've Got Mail, Let There Be Light, Movie Night, The Artist, Green Thumb,
  Deck the Halls, A Fresh Coat and Copper Age.
- **Furniture makers** also sell light switches, mailboxes, vases and presents.
- **Village workshops** have a light switch by the door and a mailbox outside.
- **Translations** for everything new in Spanish, French, German and Brazilian Portuguese.

## 1.3.0

1,459 furniture items in seventeen rooms (up from 677 in twelve), four furniture styles, new tools,
advancements, a furniture catalog, village workshops, settings and four translations.

### New rooms
- **Decor:** wallpaper in 16 colors, eight kinds of tiles, herringbone parquet floors and slabs, and
  baseboards and crown molding that form corners.
- **Garage:** pegboards that hang six tools, a workshop bench that works like a crafting table,
  toolboxes and oil drums in 16 colors, and metal shelving.
- **Library:** two-block-tall bookcases that power enchanting tables, library ladders that slide along
  the shelves, globes that spin, book stands, writing desks and a reading lamp.
- **Home bar:** bar counters that form corners, bar stools, wine racks for nine bottles, an espresso
  machine (espresso gives Haste, lattes are filling and give Speed) and a chalkboard you write on like
  a sign.
- **Nursery & hobby room:** cribs, changing tables, baby mobiles that turn, an aquarium for up to three
  fish from fish buckets, and a bird cage for the parrot on your shoulder.

### Doors, windows and art
- **French doors and sliding glass doors** in every wood; redstone opens them.
- **Windows** that join with their neighbours into one big window, with a sash that opens.
- **Picture frames** that show any item, or a filled map at full size, and **twelve new paintings**.

### Furniture styles
- **Rustic, Modern, Royal and Japanese** versions of the bed, bunk bed, dining chair, dining table,
  coffee table, nightstand, dresser, wardrobe, sofa and armchair: 480 new items with their own shapes,
  from log posts and gold trim to a futon on tatami and a shoji wardrobe. Craft them from the classic
  piece and a log, iron nugget, gold nugget or bamboo. They have their own creative tab.

### Tools
- **Furniture hammer:** turns placed furniture (sneak to turn it the other way).
- **Moving dolly:** picks up storage furniture with everything inside and puts it down elsewhere.

### Polish
- **Advancements:** a "Reiko's Furniture" tab with fifteen advancements, and a
  `reikos_furniture:furniture_action` trigger for datapacks.
- **Furniture catalog:** a book listing every piece by room and style, with its recipe and workbench
  cost. You get one with the Woodworker advancement.
- **Village workshops:** villages can have a furniture maker's workshop with a villager inside.
- **Settings:** furniture in other mods' woods (and their styles), the village workshop weight, the
  laundry cycle, appliance speed, safe ownership and what the dolly may carry. They can be changed in
  game from the Mods screen.
- **Translations:** Spanish, French, German and Brazilian Portuguese, with names that follow each
  language's grammar, also for furniture in other mods' woods.

### Changes
- Sofas now only join sofas of the same style.

## 1.2.0

677 furniture items in twelve rooms (up from 462 in eight), four new foods and drinks, and
furniture in other mods' woods.

### New rooms
- **Laundry room:** a washing machine that takes the dye out of leather armor, wool, carpet,
  banners and shulker boxes, a dryer that dries wet sponges, and laundry baskets.
- **Music room:** a piano you can play (the note follows where you click), a piano bench, a record
  player that works like a jukebox, and a drum kit.
- **Game room:** pool tables that join together, an arcade cabinet with a glowing screen and high
  scores, a dartboard that scores throws and gives a redstone signal when hit by arrows, and gaming
  chairs in 16 colors.
- **Pet corner:** pet beds that cats sit on and tamed wolves lie down on, cat trees, and feeding bowls
  that heal hurt tamed pets.

### Food and drink
- **Coffee machine, toaster and blender** for the kitchen, making coffee (Speed), hot cocoa
  (Absorption), fruit smoothies (Regeneration) and toast. Their recipes are data files, so datapacks
  can add more.

### New pieces in existing rooms
- **Item display:** wall shelves (three items), plates and cutting boards show what you put on them.
- **Lighting:** ceiling light, chandelier, pendant lamps in 16 colors and wall lamps.
- **Windows:** blinds in 16 colors and wooden shutters; both keep the light out when closed.
- **Practical pieces:** trash can, a safe only its owner can open, a wall clock that shows the time,
  and a doorbell.

### Other mods
- **Other mods' woods:** with a mod like Biomes O' Plenty installed, every wooden piece is also made
  in its woods, with that mod's textures, recipes and names.
- **JEI and EMI:** recipe pages for the furniture workbench and the kitchen appliances.

### Polish
- **Furniture maker villager:** the furniture workbench is a job site. Furniture makers trade
  planks, logs and wool for furniture.
- **Own textures and sounds:** woven fabrics for upholstered pieces, appliance fronts, animated TV,
  computer and arcade screens; sounds for the microwave, toilet, TV, doorbell, washer, dryer,
  coffee machine, toaster, blender, workbench and arcade.
- The scripts that make the model templates, textures and sounds are in `tools/`.
- Full shelves no longer hand an item back when you right-click them with an item.

## 1.1.0

462 items in eight rooms (up from 195 in four).

### New rooms
- **Bathroom:** toilet (sit, flush), bathtub that joins into longer tubs and fills with water, shower
  that puts out burning players, pedestal sink, mirror cabinet, towel racks in 16 colors.
- **Office:** office chairs in 16 colors, filing cabinets, a computer and a desk lamp.
- **Outdoor:** patio table and chairs, garden bench, planter boxes you can grow crops in, and a grill
  that cooks food on top like a campfire.
- **Kids' room:** toy chest, kids' table and chair, rocking horse, and a bunk bed.

### New pieces in existing rooms
- **Bedroom:** beds you can sleep in (dye them to change the blanket), wall mirror, vanity table.
- **Kitchen:** microwave, kitchen island, glass-door upper cabinet.
- **Dining room:** two-block-tall glass cupboard.
- **Living room:** armchairs, rugs and curtains in 16 colors, and a brick fireplace.

### Smarter furniture
- **Furniture workbench:** turns planks into any all-wood piece of that wood.
- Storage doors and drawers show as open while someone is using them.
- Sofas join into L-shapes, and counters form inner and outer corners.
- Lamps, the TV and the computer can be switched with redstone. The TV has four channels.

### Other
- License changed to MIT.
- Mod icon.
- GitHub builds the mod and runs the in-game tests on every push, and publishes a release when a
  version tag is pushed.
