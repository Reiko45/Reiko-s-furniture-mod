# Changelog

## 1.1.0

462 items in eight rooms (up from 195 in four).

### New rooms
- **Bathroom:** toilet (sit, flush), bathtub that joins into longer tubs and fills with water, shower
  that puts out burning players, pedestal sink, mirror cabinet, towel racks in 16 colors.
- **Office:** office chairs in 16 colors, filing cabinets, a computer and a desk lamp.
- **Outdoor:** patio table and chairs, garden bench, planter boxes you can grow crops in, and a grill
  that cooks food on top like a campfire.
- **Kids' room:** toy chest, kids' table and chair, rocking horse, and a bunk bed.

### New pieces in existing rooms
- **Bedroom:** beds you can sleep in (dye them to change the blanket), wall mirror, vanity table.
- **Kitchen:** microwave, kitchen island, glass-door upper cabinet.
- **Dining room:** two-block-tall glass cupboard.
- **Living room:** armchairs, rugs and curtains in 16 colors, and a brick fireplace.

### Smarter furniture
- **Furniture workbench:** turns planks into any all-wood piece of that wood.
- Storage doors and drawers show as open while someone is using them.
- Sofas join into L-shapes, and counters form inner and outer corners.
- Lamps, the TV and the computer can be switched with redstone. The TV has four channels.

### Other
- License changed to MIT.
- Mod icon.
- GitHub builds the mod and runs the in-game tests on every push, and publishes a release when a
  version tag is pushed.

## 1.0.0

First release: 195 blocks in four rooms.

- Bedroom: nightstand, dresser, wardrobe, desk, table lamp.
- Kitchen: counter, upper cabinet, sink, stove, fridge.
- Dining room: dining table, dining chair, bench, sideboard.
- Living room: sofa, coffee table, bookshelf, TV stand, TV, floor lamp.
