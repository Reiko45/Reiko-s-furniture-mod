# Reiko's Furniture

A furniture mod for **Minecraft 1.21.1** on **NeoForge**. The furniture is split into twelve room
sections: bedroom, kitchen, dining room, living room, bathroom, office, outdoor, kids' room, laundry
room, music room, game room and pet corner. Wooden pieces come in all 11 vanilla wood types (and in
the woods of other mods you have installed), and fabric pieces in all 16 colors. That makes 677
furniture items, plus four foods and drinks.

Everything is in one creative tab, **Reiko's Furniture**, ordered room by room. Each item's tooltip
shows which room it belongs to.

## How it works in game

- **Sitting:** right-click a seat with an empty hand. Sneak to stand up. Only one player fits on each
  seat.
- **Storage:** right-click to open. Doors and drawers show as open while someone is using them.
  Breaking a storage piece drops what is inside, and comparators read how full it is. Hoppers can
  put items in and take them out; the safe only lets them put items in.
- **Lights, TV and computer:** right-click to switch them on and off, or power them with redstone.
  Each right-click on the TV goes to the next of its four channels, then off.
- **Shelves, plates and cutting boards:** right-click with an item to put it down, and with an empty
  hand to take the last one back. The wall shelf holds three items, the others one.
- **Window coverings:** right-click blinds, shutters and curtains to open and close them. Closed
  blinds and shutters keep the light out.
- **Cooking:** the stove and microwave work like a smoker: they use fuel and only cook food. The
  microwave is twice as fast. The grill takes up to four pieces of raw food on top. Light it with a
  right-click or redstone, and the food pops off when it is cooked.
- **Water:** the kitchen and bathroom sinks fill empty buckets and glass bottles, and take water
  buckets back. Fill the bathtub with a water bucket and empty it with a bucket. The shower runs
  while it is switched on.
- **Beds:** beds and bunk beds work like vanilla beds: you can sleep through the night and set your
  spawn point. Right-click with any dye to change the blanket color. A bunk bed needs two free
  blocks above it, and you sleep in the lower bunk.
- **Joining pieces:** tables of the same wood join together, with legs only on the outer corners.
  Pool tables join the same way, with cushions only on the outer edges. Sofas facing the same way
  join into a long sofa, of any colors, and two rows at a right angle make an L-shaped sofa. Counters
  form inner and outer corners like stairs. Bathtubs join into longer tubs, and rugs of the same
  color join into one rug.
- **Trash can:** put things in and close the lid, and they are gone. Items hoppers put in are
  destroyed right away.
- **Safe:** it remembers who placed it. Only that player (or someone in creative mode) can open or
  break it, and explosions don't hurt it.
- **Wall clock:** the hands follow the time of day. Right-click it to see the time.
- **Doorbell:** press it like a button. It rings and gives a short redstone pulse.
- **Planter box:** plant flowers, saplings or crops on top. Crops grow as fast as on watered
  farmland.
- **Fireplace:** light it with flint and steel or a right-click. It doesn't spread fire.
- **Bookshelf:** counts as a bookshelf for enchanting tables.
- **Washing machine and dryer:** put things in and close the door (or fill it with a hopper). The
  machine runs for five seconds. The washing machine takes the color out of leather armor, colored
  wool and carpet, and shulker boxes, and removes the top pattern of banners. The dryer turns wet
  sponges into dry ones.
- **Piano and drum kit:** right-click the piano's keys. Further left plays lower notes, further right
  higher ones, over two octaves. The drum kit plays a bass drum, snare or hi-hat depending on how high
  you hit it.
- **Record player:** works like a jukebox. Right-click with a music disc to play it, and with an
  empty hand to take it out. Comparators read it like a jukebox.
- **Arcade cabinet:** right-click to switch it on and get a new high score. Redstone switches it too.
- **Dartboard:** right-click to throw a dart and see your score. Arrows and other projectiles that
  hit it score too, and give a redstone signal for a second, like a target block.
- **Pets:** cats sit on pet beds and on top of cat trees. Idle tamed wolves walk over to a pet bed
  nearby and lie down on it. Fill a feeding bowl with up to 16 meat or fish: hurt tamed wolves and
  cats nearby eat from it and heal.
- Furniture faces you when placed, and most pieces can be placed underwater (waterlogged). Wooden
  and wool furniture can burn, except crimson and warped wood.

## Food and drink

The coffee machine, toaster and blender make food. Right-click an appliance with the ingredients
(and the glass bottle for drinks) to put them in. When they match a recipe the appliance starts,
and the result pops out when it is done. Right-click with an empty hand to take the ingredients back
before it starts.

| Food | Made in | Ingredients | Time | Effect |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Coffee | Coffee machine | Cocoa beans, glass bottle | 5 s | Speed for 30 seconds |
| Hot cocoa | Coffee machine | Cocoa beans, milk bucket, glass bottle | 6 s | Absorption for 30 seconds |
| Fruit smoothie | Blender | Two fruits (apple, sweet berries, glow berries or melon slice), glass bottle | 5 s | Regeneration for 5 seconds |
| Toast | Toaster | Bread | 4 s | More filling than bread |

Drinks give the glass bottle back. The recipes are data files (recipe type
`reikos_furniture:appliance`), so a datapack can add or change them.

## Furniture workbench

The workbench works like a stonecutter for furniture:

1. Put planks of any wood in the left slot.
2. Click a piece. Pieces you don't have enough planks for are faded, and the tooltip shows the cost.
3. Take the piece from the right slot. Shift-click to make as many as the planks allow.

It makes every all-wood piece in the wood you put in. Pieces that need other materials (lamps,
beds, sinks, counters, bookshelves, glass pieces and so on) are made at a crafting table.

## Furniture maker villager

The furniture workbench is a job site. An unemployed villager near one becomes a **furniture
maker**, with a canvas apron and a pencil behind the ear. Furniture makers buy planks, logs and wool,
and sell furniture: chairs, table lamps, rugs and coffee tables at the first levels, then floor lamps,
bookshelves, sofas, beds and record players, and at Master level TVs, fridges, arcade cabinets and
pianos.

## Recipe viewers (JEI and EMI)

If you have [JEI](https://www.curseforge.com/minecraft/mc-mods/jei) or
[EMI](https://modrinth.com/mod/emi) installed, they show two extra recipe pages: **Furniture
Workbench** (the planks each piece costs) and **Kitchen Appliances** (what each appliance makes, from
what, and how long it takes). Crafting table recipes show up as usual. Neither mod is needed to play.

## Furniture in other mods' woods

When you play with mods that add their own wood (for example Biomes O' Plenty), every wooden piece
is also made in those woods: Fir Nightstand, Redwood Bunk Bed, and so on. They have the same
recipes as the vanilla versions, with that wood's planks, logs and slabs, they can be made at the
furniture workbench, and they come at the end of the creative tab.

This works for any mod whose wood has `<wood>_planks`, `<wood>_log` (or `_stem`) and `<wood>_slab`
blocks. The models use that mod's textures. Their models, loot tables, recipes and names are made at
startup from the oak versions, in a hidden built-in pack. As always, the server and every player need
the same mods.

## Building and running

You need Java 21. The Gradle wrapper downloads everything else.

```bash
./gradlew build
```

The mod jar is written to `build/libs/reikos_furniture-1.2.0.jar`. Put it in the `mods` folder of a
Minecraft 1.21.1 NeoForge install.

Other useful tasks:

| Command | What it does |
|---|---|
| `./gradlew runClient` | Starts the game with the mod loaded |
| `./gradlew runServer` | Starts a dedicated server with the mod loaded |
| `./gradlew runData` | Regenerates models, block states, recipes, loot tables, tags, sounds and names into `src/generated/resources` |
| `./gradlew runGameTestServer` | Runs the automated in-game tests and exits |

Run `./gradlew runData` after changing pieces, models or recipes, and commit the files it generates.

To try the mod with JEI, EMI or a wood mod in the dev client, put their jars in `run/mods`.

## Automatic builds and releases

GitHub Actions runs on every push and pull request. It builds the mod, runs the in-game tests and
checks that `src/generated` is up to date. The jar is attached to each run as a download.

To publish a release:

1. Set `mod_version` in `gradle.properties`, for example `1.2.0`.
2. Push a tag with the same version:
   ```bash
   git tag v1.2.0
   ```
   ```bash
   git push origin v1.2.0
   ```
3. GitHub builds and tests the mod and creates a release with the jar attached.

To publish on Modrinth or CurseForge, create a project there with your own account. Upload the jar
from the GitHub release, use `src/main/resources/logo.png` as the icon, and paste the release notes
from `CHANGELOG.md`. Pick NeoForge as the loader and 1.21.1 as the game version.

## Project layout

```

  ReikosFurnitureClient.java   Client-only setup (renderers, screens, colors)
  core/                        Shared parts every room uses
    FurnitureWood.java         The wood types (the vanilla ones, plus any found in other mods)
    FurnitureRegistry.java     Registers furniture blocks and their items
    block/                     Base blocks: seats, storage, two-block-tall pieces, lights, tables, beds
    blockentity/, entity/      Storage inventory, invisible seat entity
    display/                   Blocks that show items (shelves, plates, feeding bowls) and their renderer
    modded/                    Furniture in other mods' woods: detection and the built-in pack
    registry/                  Block entities, sounds, recipe types, the creative tab
    workbench/                 Furniture workbench block, recipe type, menu and screen
  bedroom/  kitchen/  dining/  livingroom/  bathroom/  office/  outdoor/  kids/
  laundry/  music/  gameroom/  pets/
    <Room>Furniture.java       The room's blocks
    <Room>Data.java            The room's models and recipes (used by runData)
  kitchen/appliance/           Coffee machine, toaster, blender and their recipe type
  villager/                    The furniture maker profession and trades
  compat/                      JEI and EMI plugins (only loaded when those mods are installed)
  datagen/                     Data generation shared by all rooms
  gametest/                    Automated in-game tests
src/main/resources/assets/reikos_furniture/
  models/block/template/<room>/  Model templates (written by tools/models)
  textures/                    The mod's own textures (written by tools/textures)
  sounds/                      The mod's own sounds (written by tools/sounds)

```

Each piece has template models that use the texture variables `#planks`, `#log` and `#stripped_log`
(fabric pieces use `#wool`). Data generation creates the model for every wood type or color from
them. You can open the templates in [Blockbench](https://www.blockbench.net/) as Java block models.

## Tools

The templates, textures and sounds are made by Python scripts, so they can be changed and made
again. Run them from the repository root. Each one writes its files into `src/main/resources`;
commit the results.

| Script | Needs | What it does |
|---|---|---|
| `python3 tools/models/generate_all.py` | Python 3 | Rewrites every model template from the room scripts in `tools/models/` |
| `python3 tools/textures/generate_textures.py` | Pillow | Draws the fabrics, appliance fronts, screens, food items and villager clothes |
| `python3 tools/sounds/generate_sounds.py` | numpy, ffmpeg | Makes the sounds (microwave ding, toilet flush, washer, ...) as `.ogg` files |
| `python3 tools/check_textures.py` | Python 3 | Checks that every texture a model uses exists (run `./gradlew build` first) |

To change a piece's shape, edit its room script in `tools/models/` (for example `tools/models/office.py`).
Each part is a `Box((x1, y1, z1), (x2, y2, z2), texture)` in pixels, with the front facing north.
Then run `generate_all.py` and `./gradlew runData`.

### Adding a piece to a room

1. Add the block to the room's `<Room>Furniture.java`, for example:
   ```java
   public static final WoodSet<FurnitureStorageBlock> CHEST_OF_DRAWERS = FurnitureRegistry.woodSet(
           "chest_of_drawers", ROOM, wood -> new FurnitureStorageBlock(wood.properties(), 3, SHAPE));
   ```
2. Add its template models to the room's script in `tools/models/` (here `chest_of_drawers` and
   `chest_of_drawers_open`, front facing north) and run `generate_all.py`.
3. In `<Room>Data.java`, add the models (`models.woodFacingOpenable(CHEST_OF_DRAWERS, "chest_of_drawers")`)
   and a recipe. To make it at the workbench too, add its plank cost to `datagen/CarpentryCosts.java`.
4. Run `./gradlew runData`.

Names, loot tables and mining tags are generated automatically, and the piece is made in other
mods' woods too.

### Adding a room

1. Add the room to `core/Room.java`.
2. Create a package for it with a `<Room>Furniture` class and a `<Room>Data` class, like the existing
   rooms, and a script for its models in `tools/models/` (add it to the list in `generate_all.py`).
3. Call `<Room>Furniture.init()` in `ReikosFurniture` and add `new <Room>Data()` to the list in
   `datagen/DataGenerators.java`.
